================================================================

1. CRITICAL DOWNWARD LAUNCH ANGLE
At the limiting ray, theta=0 at y=0, so n(y0) cos(theta_c) = n(0).
theta_c = arccos(n(0)/n(y0)).
Launch height y0 = 0.300000 m
n(0) = 1.000243741708
n(y0) = 1.000268815673
Theoretical critical angle magnitude = 0.405687553 deg
Numerical critical-angle sample minimum height = 9.691549232e-07 m at x = 70.552929 m
Nearest sampled turning ray below threshold = 0.405281866 deg
Nearest sampled ground-hit ray above threshold = 0.406093241 deg

                  0.0 | ground impact at x=57.295256 m | min y=0.000000000
